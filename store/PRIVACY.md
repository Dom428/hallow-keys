# Hallow Keys browser extension - privacy policy (draft)

Last updated: (date of publishing)

The Hallow Keys extension is a companion to the Hallow Keys app for Windows. It has no server and collects nothing.

- **What it handles:** logins, 2FA codes, card details and addresses that you choose to fill, and logins you choose to save. They pass
  only between the page you are on and the Hallow Keys app on the same PC, through the browser's native messaging (a local channel).
- **What leaves your PC:** nothing. The extension makes no network requests of its own, has no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
- **What it stores in the browser:** its own settings, and for up to 5 minutes the name of the login you picked on the first page of a
  two-step sign-in. No passwords, cards or addresses are stored by the extension.
- **What it reads from pages:** the form boxes on the page (their names, labels and types) to know what to fill. Page content is never
  collected or sent anywhere.
- **Sharing:** none. Nothing is sold or given to anyone.
- **Your vault:** lives in the Hallow Keys app on your PC, encrypted (AES-256-GCM). See https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-keys.
